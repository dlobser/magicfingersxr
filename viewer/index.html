FROM YOUR HANDS TO YOUR HARD DRIVE

Bring the magic home.

  1. In Magic Fingers, open the hand menu and go to Style → Your shapes (your saved reset shapes). Recall a saved shape if needed.
  2. Choose Export point cloud for dots, or Export spheres for solid geometry. This exports the particles as they are now. Strands become tubes and flocking creatures keep their shapes.
  3. Connect your headset to your computer with USB and allow file access. Use a headset file manager such as SideQuest, or ADB, to copy the Exports folder to your computer.
  4. Click Add PLY / OBJ files above, or drop the files into the viewer. Select your saved files from the collection dropdown.

Headset folder:

/sdcard/Android/data/com.lightclinic.magicfingers/files/Exports

With an ADB connection already set up:

adb pull /sdcard/Android/data/com.lightclinic.magicfingers/files/Exports

If your file manager cannot access Android/data, use ADB. Browser storage is local to this browser and website, not a backup; keep the exported files on your computer. OBJ geometry and embedded vertex colors are supported; external MTL files and textures are not imported.

Keyboard: arrows orbit, Shift + arrows pan, + / − zoom, F fits. On touch screens: one finger orbits, two fingers pan and pinch to zoom.

Rendering powered by three-gpu-pathtracer and Three.js. Path tracing requires WebGL 2 and float render targets.
